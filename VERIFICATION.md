# Public evidence and claim boundaries

This file maps the externally verifiable statements in the [profile README](README.md) to their sources. A GitHub repository is evidence of **published code and documented tests**, not proof of real-world deployment, scientific accuracy, or third-party certification. Research roles and awards are described only to the extent supported by the linked record. An inaccessible or generic page is not adequate evidence of individual involvement. Academic-grade and specific employment assertions not independently verified are deliberately omitted.

**Evidence policy:** A source proving that a research project exists does not by itself prove a person's contractual involvement, assigned responsibilities, or implementation of its software. Publications prove authorship, not ownership of an entire project. Where an achievement has no specific public corroboration yet, it is omitted from the public profile rather than presented as independently verified.
