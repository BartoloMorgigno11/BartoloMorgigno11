A GitHub repository is evidence of **published code and documented tests**, not proof of real-world deployment, scientific accuracy, or third-party certification. Research roles and awards are described only to the extent supported by the linked record. An inaccessible or generic page is not adequate evidence of individual involvement. Academic dates and qualifications are based on the owner's declarations and linked public education profile where accessible; their public pages may not show every detail. Signed contracts were privately inspected to verify appointment types and scopes, but are **not uploaded or linked** because they contain personal and contractual information.

**Evidence policy:** A source proving that a research project exists does not by itself prove a person's contractual involvement, assigned responsibilities, or implementation of its software. Publications prove authorship, not ownership of an entire project. Private signed agreements can establish a factual appointment for its holder, but they are **not independently accessible to a public GitHub visitor**. Where a named public award is not yet linked, its source is marked as a privately reviewed contract, not described as an externally accessible verification. Internal reports on ReFood and TRAFILCOOP are not exposed or quoted as independently published scientific validation.
