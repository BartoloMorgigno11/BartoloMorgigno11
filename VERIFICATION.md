**Evidence policy:** A source proving that a research project exists does not by itself prove a person's contractual involvement, assigned responsibilities, or implementation of its software. Publications prove authorship, not ownership of an entire project. Where an achievement has no specific public corroboration yet, it is omitted from the public profile rather than presented as independently verified.
