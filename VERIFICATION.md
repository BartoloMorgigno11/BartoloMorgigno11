**Evidence policy:** A source proving that a research project exists does not by itself prove a person's contractual involvement, assigned responsibilities, or implementation of its software. Publications prove authorship, not ownership of an entire project. Private signed agreements can establish a factual appointment for its holder, but they are **not independently accessible to a public GitHub visitor**. Where a named public award is not yet linked, its source is marked as a privately reviewed contract, not described as an externally accessible verification. Internal reports on ReFood and TRAFILCOOP are not exposed or quoted as independently published scientific validation.
